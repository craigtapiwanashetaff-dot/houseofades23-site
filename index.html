Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the usual questions.

How do I book?

Fill in the booking form above and send the request. You can also message House of Fades directly on WhatsApp, Instagram or SMS.

Is my appointment confirmed straight away?

No. The form sends a request. House of Fades confirms the date and time with you using the contact method you choose.

What is the difference between the services?

Standard Haircut is the cut only. Haircut + Enhancements and Haircut + Dye/Tint use products you bring yourself. Full Service uses House of Fades products for the whole job.

Do you take bookings outside Orange?

Yes. Requests are open across NSW, depending on your location. Add your suburb and postcode and you will be told if it can be done.

How do I pay?

Payment options are confirmed with you when your appointment is confirmed.
